Setu Student Support Services

SETU Student Support Services provides a range of supports that are focused on helping students both during the transition to third level and throughout their time in South East Technological University. The Student Support team works with students on a wide variety of issues, including personal and academic concerns. If the team can’t provide you with assistance, they will certainly point you in the direction of someone who can.

SETU Carlow Campus Medical Center SETU Carlow Campus Medical Centre is a nurse-led service provided by an Advanced Nurse Practitioner (ANP) and is available to all full-time registered students and apprentices during college hours throughout the academic year. The service is a term-time addition and not a replacement for the student’s own GP or medical service. It provides acute care during the week when students are unable to attend their usual medical service and operates within defined resource constraints. The service offers on-site assessment, diagnosis, prescribing, treatment, and management of students’ health needs; referral to hospital and community-based services when required; and promotion of positive wellbeing through advice, information, and health promotion. Doctors attend the Medical Centre four days per week (Monday - Thursday) term-time only. Doctor appointments are booked by phone, on the day, from 09.00-10.30. Telephone: +353 (0) 59 9175606. Medical records are private and confidential and are not part of the main SETU records.
